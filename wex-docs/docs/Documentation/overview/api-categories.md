Our APIs are organized into the following categories:

### Communication


Enable communication between your platform and WEX, including notifications, messages, and document exchange.

### Person


Manage person-related data, including demographics, contact information, and employment details.

### Identity


Verify and authenticate user identities securely.

### Plans/Products


Access and manage information about benefit plans and financial products.

### Claims


Submit, track, and manage claims.

### Balances/Cards


Retrieve and update account balances and card information.

### Business/Employer


Manage business and employer data, including company profiles and consumer details.
